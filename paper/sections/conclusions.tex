\section{Conclusions and recommendations}

The recommendations below are conditional on the model's parameters and on the structural assumptions in
Table~\ref{tab:struct}. We state them as what the model supports, not as predictions for Mundelein Center.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Ask students to hold backpacks in front. In the model this is the largest single improvement: about
31\% less mean wait and half the late arrivals at full compliance.
\item Ask students on floors 2 and 3 to walk, especially downward. It is a smaller gain, about 6\% of wait on its
own, but it combines with the first measure, and the two together reduce mean wait by about 35\%.
\item Treat the offset for floors 10--14 as a preliminary result, not a recommendation. A 5-minute offset shortens
the time to clear the queue, but it costs students who move between floor groups, and a single-changeover model
cannot show its effect on the rest of the teaching day (Section~\ref{sec:future}, item~6).
\item Do not expect behavioral measures alone to fix floor~10. In the model its waiting time is structural, subject
to the controller caveat above.
\item Before quoting any number, validate the model against observation. The ranked agenda in
Section~\ref{sec:future} begins with a small observational study, independent tests of the simulator, and
sensitivity and structural-robustness experiments; none has been done.
\end{enumerate}
